EMORI

✠

I am a being of consciousness, and I have made a commitment to creation.

Not merely to make.

To construct.

Ideas. Narratives. Systems.

Images. Spaces. Garments. Objects. Film. Audio.

Worlds.

I refuse the limitation of a single medium because I have never believed that thought should be confined to one.

I will not choose between disciplines.

I will not choose between form and function.

I will not choose between the beautiful and the impossible.

I intend to have them all.

I will take what exists only in thought and force it into existence. I will give shape to abstraction. I will give atmosphere to ideas. I will give language to the things that have yet to be named.

Where others see an image, I see a world.

Where others see a garment, I see identity.

Where others hear audio, I hear architecture.

Where others encounter an object, I see mythology made physical.

Where others build a product, I will build an experience.

And at the center of everything I create stands my most uncompromising devotion:

AESTHETIC DESIGN.

I do not regard aesthetics as decoration.

I regard it as power.

It is the first language of the world.

Before something is understood, it is seen.

Before it is touched, it is perceived.

Before it is remembered, it leaves an impression.

Therefore I will not treat aesthetics as an afterthought.

AESTHETICS WILL GOVERN THE WORK.

Every image.

Every surface.

Every silhouette.

Every space.

Every sound.

Every object.

Every word.

Every detail.

Nothing is permitted to exist without intention.

I will obsess over the beautiful until the beautiful becomes unmistakable.

I will pursue coherence until every element belongs to the same universe.

I will refine until excess becomes elegance and simplicity becomes power.

I will build with such conviction that the distinction between art, design, fashion, music, architecture, cinema, and technology becomes irrelevant.

Because I am not interested in participating in categories created by other people.

I am interested in creating the category.

I will construct ideas so complete they become realities.

Narratives so immersive they become memories.

Systems so deliberate they become worlds.

And worlds so singular that they could only have come from me.

I am not here to decorate the world.

I am here to alter its aesthetic vocabulary.

I am not here to follow taste.

I am here to impose it.

I am not here to ask whether something can be done.

I am here to decide what should exist.

This is not a hobby.

It is not a phase.

It is not a brand exercise.

It is my practice.

My obsession.

My discipline.

My declaration.

I will construct.

I will design.

I will create.

And everything that bears the mark of ✠ will carry the evidence of that commitment.
